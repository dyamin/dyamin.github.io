At [Chaos Labs](https://chaoslabs.xyz/) (2024–2025) I was a senior software engineer on the backend of an automated **economic security platform for DeFi protocols** — world-class risk simulation used by protocols such as **Aave, Uniswap, and Osmosis** to manage risk on billions of dollars in digital assets.

## What I built

- **Low-latency backend systems** feeding the real-time risk platform — where the value of a risk signal decays in seconds, not minutes.
- **Decentralized data paths** that let protocols consume dynamic, risk-reducing parameters directly inside their smart contracts, in real time.

## What made it interesting

DeFi risk is anomaly detection with adversaries and money on the line: market conditions shift violently, attacks are engineered to look like noise, and the system's outputs are consumed autonomously by smart contracts. It rewards exactly the combination I like — algorithmic thinking, distributed systems discipline, and healthy paranoia.
